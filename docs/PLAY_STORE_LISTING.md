# Google Play listing draft

**App name:** PrintGo – Print from Phone
**Short description (80):** Print photos, PDFs and web pages to Wi-Fi printers – no ads, no account.

**Full description**
PrintGo prints from your phone to almost any Wi-Fi printer – no cables, no extra apps, no account.

• Photos & images – choose one or many, pick paper size, borders and colour
• Documents – PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text files
• Web pages – print any article or page
• Share from any app – tap Share → PrintGo
• Smart layout – copies, page ranges, 2/4/6/9 pages per sheet, two-sided, scale, margins, rotate, watermark and page numbers
• Auto discovery – finds AirPrint / Mopria / IPP printers on your Wi-Fi, or add one by IP address
• Live job status – progress, cancel, retry, and clear messages such as "out of paper"
• Save as PDF when you have no printer
• Print history with search, reprint and CSV export
• Private by design – your files go only to your printer. No ads. No tracking.

**Category:** Tools   **Content rating:** Everyone   **Contains ads:** No

**Data safety form:** No data collected, no data shared. Data is processed on device and sent only to the user-selected printer.

**Permissions to declare**
- Foreground service (data sync): keeps print jobs running when the app is in the background.
- Nearby devices / Bluetooth: connect to user-paired Bluetooth printers.
- Notifications: job result alerts.
