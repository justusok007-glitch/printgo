# PrintGo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

PrintGo does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

**Your files.** Photos and documents you choose are read on your phone, converted to pages, and sent only to the printer you select (over your local network, Bluetooth or USB) or saved as a PDF in your Downloads folder. A copy of each prepared job is kept in PrintGo's private storage so you can retry or reprint it; it is deleted when the job leaves your history.

**History.** PrintGo remembers the name, time, printer and settings of your print jobs on your phone for 30 days (changeable in Settings). History leaves your phone only if you export it yourself.

**Printers and network.** PrintGo uses Wi-Fi discovery (mDNS) on your local network to find printers. Printer addresses and trusted certificate fingerprints are stored on your phone. Web pages are downloaded only when you ask PrintGo to print a web address.

**Permissions.** Network/Wi-Fi state – find and talk to printers. Nearby devices/Bluetooth – connect to Bluetooth printers you pair. Notifications – tell you when a job finishes. Photos – let you pick pictures to print. Foreground service – keep sending a job while you switch apps.

**Children.** PrintGo collects no information from anyone, including children.

**Contact.** justusok007@gmail.com

The same text is shown inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy. To use it for the Play Store, publish this file (for example with GitHub Pages) and enter the public link in Play Console.
